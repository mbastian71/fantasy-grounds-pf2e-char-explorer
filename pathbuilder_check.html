Rules audit
Deviations compares the two exports by proficiency rank (untrained/trained/expert/master/legendary), ability score, and item/misc bonus. Feats are matched by name and may show false differences from Remaster renames. Rules audit reads the Fantasy Grounds XML alone, checking it against PF2e core rules and the Strength of Thousands Magaambya academia subsystem; branch levels come from the character's notes. Descriptions and rules text are never compared.
